The Experience

Churrasco the way it’s done in Brazil, right in your backyard.

We bring the rotisserie, the coals and the craft. Premium cuts are seasoned with coarse salt, turned slowly over open fire and carved fresh for your guests, with the warmth of a Brazilian family table.

  • 01

    Live fire

    Real coals and a rotating skewer grill, set up on site. Nothing is reheated.

  • 02

    Premium cuts

    Picanha, filet mignon, ribs and more, grilled in the traditional Brazilian style.

  • 03

    You host, we grill

    We handle the fire and the service so you can enjoy the moment with your guests.

Specialty Cuts

Cortes nobres.
Premium cuts, fire-kissed.

  1. Picanha

    Top Sirloin Cap

    The crown jewel of churrasco. A thick fat cap renders over the coals for a crisp edge and juicy center.

  2. Fraldinha

    Flank Steak

    Loose-grained and full of flavor, sliced thin against the grain.

  3. Filé Mignon

    Beef Tenderloin

    Butter-tender and mild, a favorite at every table.

  4. Costela

    Beef Ribs

    Slow-roasted until the meat pulls cleanly from the bone.

  5. Alcatra & Contra-filé

    Top Sirloin & Strip Steak

    Classic Brazilian steakhouse cuts with a generous sear.
